The Café

Where the day begins

Tall windows, warm plaster and the hiss of the steam wand — our café is the front door to the house. Morning pastries land on the counter at half past seven, the espresso is pulled from a rotating single origin, and the regulars are known by name.

Take a seat by the window with a cortado and the paper, or a corner table for a long, unhurried lunch.

The Chicken House

Built around the fire & the spit

Our kitchen is built around the fire and the spit. Free-range birds are brined overnight in buttermilk and bay, roasted slowly over hardwood embers, then carved to order and finished with a spoonful of pan jus.

Alongside the rotisserie: hot-honey fried chicken, duck-fat potatoes, and a pot pie worth the wait.

The Bakery

A levain kept alive since day one

A wood-fired oven, a wild starter we have fed every morning since we opened, and flour from a single mill. Our bread is long-fermented, baked dark, and best torn apart while it is still warm.

Croissants are laminated by hand, the tart cases are lined with almond cream, and anything unsold at close goes to the neighbourhood fridge.
